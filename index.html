How the numbers are worked out
  • Question order follows the Tool Design sheet, which the bots are configured from. Q numbers are the sheet's own numbers.
  • Answered means the main column or its follow-up column has text. "Not answered yet" counts as blank. For State and District, "Others" counts as answered only when a name was typed.
  • Got to this question means the person answered the question just before it, or any later one. Stopped here means they got to this question but answered nothing from here on. Drop % = stopped here ÷ got to this question.
  • Finished means the person answered the last question in the bot order.
  • Age screen-outs are set aside: age outside the tool's range (Youth 12–25, General Public 18 and over) and nothing answered after age. The bot ends there, so they are not drop-offs.
  • A question that is empty for every session in the export is shown in grey and not counted as a step, because no drop can be measured there.
  • Answer quality: every answer was read and rated No response, Off-topic, Vague, Clear or Rich (17 Nov rating). "Clear or rich" is the share rated Clear or Rich. "After follow-up" also reads the reply to Mitra's follow-up question.
  • Parent answer quality is rated on a random 1,000 parents, so district figures for parents rest on fewer people. Other groups are rated in full.
  • Place comes from the State and District answers. Sessions in the Others table only have typed text; a typed name that matches a listed state or district is used, anything else shows as "Other (typed)", and no answer shows as "Not given".
  • State and district are asked near the end, so anyone who left earlier is under "Not given". Finish rates for a named place only cover people who reached that question.
  • Numbers based on fewer than 30 people are shown faded. Read them with care.
  • Google Form answers have no matching place field here, so they are shown for all India only.
