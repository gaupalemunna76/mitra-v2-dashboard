How the numbers are worked out
  • Who is counted. These are left out of every drop-out figure and listed in table A: test sessions (every open question answered "skip", 6 or more skips), exact repeats of another session on the same day, age screen-outs (age outside the tool's range, Youth 12–25 and General Public 18+, with nothing answered after age; the bot ends the survey there), and people outside the age range who answered on anyway.
  • Step order. The Tool Design sheet gives the Q numbers. The order was checked by counting people who left a question blank but answered a later one; where the answers show the bot asks in another order, that order is used.
  • Answered. The main answer or the reply to Mitra's follow-up has text. An empty follow-up reply does not count. "Skip", "next" or "submit" counts as staying in the survey but not as an answer.
  • State is a step where the bot asks it in the flow; choosing "Others" and typing nothing counts as no answer. District is never a step, because "Others" in the Main table cannot be told apart from no answer.
  • Conditional questions (asked only to some people) are not steps. They are shown under the step table as answered out of the people they apply to.
  • Got to this question = answered the step before it or any later one. Stopped here = got to it but answered nothing from here on. Drop % = stopped here ÷ got to it. Opened but answered nothing is its own line, not a drop at the first question.
  • Finished = answered the last step. The bot's own "Completed" status is shown next to it as a check; the two agree for almost every session.
  • Answer quality. Every answer was read and rated No response, Off-topic, Vague, Clear or Rich. Clear or rich is the headline. "After follow-up" also reads the reply to Mitra's follow-up. Only counted sessions are used, and "skip" answers are left out.
  • Choice questions (for example "very confident / somewhat / unsure") are judged only on whether a usable choice was given, and are not in the headline. Parents 3.0 shows two measures: a satisfaction level given, and a reason given (after follow-up).
  • Every answer from every session, in all four groups and in the Google Form, was read and rated (full rating, 19 Nov). All answers were rated in the same run by the same raters with one written rubric, so groups, questions and districts are comparable. A blind re-rating of 300 answers agreed within one level 99% of the time.
  • Place comes from the State and District answers. A typed name that matches a listed state or district is used; anything else is "Other (typed)"; no answer is "Not given". Place is not recorded for everyone who left early, so the finish rate of a named place covers mostly people who stayed longer.
  • Numbers based on fewer than 30 people are faded. Google Form answers have no matching place field here, so they are shown for all India only; Google Form choice answers are rated the same way as Mitra ones.
